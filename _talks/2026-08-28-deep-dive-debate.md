How and when should AI be used in research? And how might research quality be affected by Anthropic's recent announcement to embed invisible watermarks into text written by its AI assistant Claude?

Kamil Szostak, lawyer and computer scientist, gave a live demo of how "vibecoding" allows the rapid generation of promising research prototypes and can help literature research in CS&Law, followed by an open debate moderated by Konrad Kollnig.

Suggested reading: [Can Anthropic's invisible watermarks curb 'AI slop'?](https://www.nature.com/articles/d41586-026-02503-7) (13 August 2026).
